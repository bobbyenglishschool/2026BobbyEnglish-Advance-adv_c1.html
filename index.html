💡 教學理念：關於現階段的英語思維 | Educational Philosophy

【將語言實力轉化為深刻的觀點表達】 [Turning Language Skills into Meaningful, Well-Expressed Viewpoints]

對於目前就讀國三、且已具備英檢初級以上實力的同學而言，這個階段的英語學習，關鍵在於「如何將語言能力轉化為深刻的觀點表達」。在明年五月迎來國中會考之前，孩子們除了需要穩固嚴謹的語法結構以應對長難句，更需要提早適應高中、甚至未來國際頂尖學制所重視的跨學科思辨能力。本課程於 8/31 至隔年 1/4 的每週一晚上，以全美語的沉浸式環境，陪伴 10 人以內的精緻班級，進行一場視野與筆尖的跨界探索。（※ 備註：9/28、10/26 國定假日不上課。）

【Developing Critical Academic Thought】 For 9th graders who already possess intermediate-level English proficiency, the key to language development at this stage lies in turning language skills into meaningful, well-expressed viewpoints. Before facing the upcoming CAP exam next May, students not only need a solid grasp of rigorous grammatical structures to handle complex reading passages but also need early exposure to the interdisciplinary critical thinking highly valued in high school and global academic frameworks. This course runs every Monday evening from August 31st to January 4th in a fully immersive English environment. We welcome a small group of students to join us in exploring global outlooks and structural writing. (※ Note: No classes on the public holidays of Sep 28 and Oct 26.)

🌐 什麼是 IBDP 國際文憑先修？它如何幫助孩子？ | What is IBDP & How Does It Help?

許多家長常問：為什麼我們要在國三這個階段，帶入 IBDP（國際文憑課程） 的元素？IBDP 是全球頂尖大學（如哈佛、牛津、台大、清大）高度認可的菁英學制。它不考死記硬背的選擇題，而是要求學生針對全球議題進行深度閱讀，並寫出具備個人觀點的論述。提早接觸這套學制，將帶給孩子四大核心優勢：

IBDP is an elite educational framework recognized by top universities worldwide. Instead of rote memorization, it requires students to read deeply and articulate independent viewpoints. Early exposure to this framework grants students four core advantages:
  • 1. 超前演練高中「學習歷程檔案」：提前適應台灣高中 108 課綱最重視的「探究與實作」與小論文撰寫邏輯。
    High School Portfolio Readiness: Early training for the "Inquiry and Practice" projects and micro-essays highly valued in Taiwan's high school curriculum.
  • 2. 輕鬆駕馭高中英文長難句：高中英文的長度與廣度是國中的數倍，接觸學術文本能讓孩子上高中後「降維打擊」校內課業。
    Effortless Transition to High School English: Academic texts prepare students to comfortably handle the vast expansion of reading volume in high school.
  • 3. 鍛鍊個人申請的面試表達力：在 10 人小班中引導孩子針對議題進行「有邏輯的英文辯證」，培養未來甄選面試的從容自信。
    Polishing Interview & Presentation Skills: Guided logical discussions in a small group build confidence and articulation for future school interviews.
  • 4. 奠定國際檢定（托福/雅思）實力：文本的廣度涵蓋科學與人文，為未來有出國規劃或頂尖大學外文檢定門檻做最紮實的準備。
    Solid Foundation for TOEFL/IELTS: Broad content covering sciences and humanities thoroughly prepares students for future international proficiency exams.

Azar Understanding (藍本)
嚴謹語法解構與會考應戰實力		 「用最嚴謹的語法架構，輕鬆駕馭升學長難句」 "Mastering rigorous grammar structures to effortlessly navigate complex academic reading."

  • 課堂現場寫作：每週配合文法進度，引導學生在課堂上直接完成段落練習，並給予即時回饋。
    In-Class Writing: Aligned with the grammar timeline, students practice paragraph writing in class with immediate teacher feedback.

📅 17堂課學期進度總覽 | 17-Lesson Course Outline

每堂課 2 小時完美融合：45分鐘全球議題研討 ＋ 45分鐘 Azar 高階語法拆解 ＋ 30分鐘寫作與哲學反思（TOK）。
Each 2-hour session integrates: 45 mins Global Theme Discussion + 45 mins Azar Advanced Grammar + 30 mins Writing & Critical Reflection (TOK).

  • Phase 1：身分認同與高階時態 (Lessons 1-3 | Aug 31 - Sep 14)
    • 主題思辨：探討青少年次文化、語言與文化的根源、以及面對大考的心理健康調適。Global Themes: Youth subcultures, linguistic heritage, and stress-coping mechanisms for exam takers.
    • 高階語法：英文時態系統深度解析、狀態動詞與動態動詞細微差別、主謂一致性（進階複數）。Grammar Mastery: Deep dive into the tense system, stative vs. dynamic verbs, and advanced subject-verb agreement.
  • Phase 2：人生經歷與被動助動詞 (Lessons 4-5 | Sep 21 - Oct 5)
    • 主題思辨：跨文化成年禮、過度觀光與生態旅遊的平衡、全球公民意識。Global Themes: Cross-cultural rites of passage, overtourism vs. ecotourism, and global citizenship.
    • 高階語法：過去時間的助動詞推測（must/should have V3）、複雜與狀態被動語態。Grammar Mastery: Modals of past certainty/regret, complex and stative passive voice structures.
  • Phase 3：人類創造力與三大高級子句 (Lessons 6-8 | Oct 12 - Nov 2)
    • 主題思辨：AI 科技與未來職場、現代藝術與圖像閱讀（Banksy 作品分析）、基因編輯的科學倫理。Global Themes: AI technology & the future of work, visual literacy in modern street art, and ethics in gene editing.
    • 高階語法：名詞子句（含萬用虛主詞）、形容詞子句（介系詞＋關代）、副詞子句與邏輯連接詞。Grammar Mastery: Noun clauses (subjunctives), adjective clauses (prep + relative), and adverb clauses.
  • Phase 4：社會組織與進階修飾語 (Lessons 9-11 | Nov 9 - Nov 23)
    • 主題思辨：全球教育體制對比（學科考試 vs. 多元評量）、青少年司法制度、遠端工作與高齡化社會。Global Themes: Global education models (exams vs. assessments), juvenile justice, and remote work impacts.
    • 高階語法：動名詞與不定詞的語意轉變、平行結構與不對稱句型修飾。Grammar Mastery: Gerunds & infinitives with meaning changes, parallel structures, and advanced modifiers.
  • Phase 5：共享地球與高級倒裝句型 (Lessons 12-14 | Nov 30 - Dec 14)
    • 主題思辨：氣候變遷與環境難民、快時尚背後的資源消耗、國際非政府組織（NGO）的全球合作。Global Themes: Climate change and eco-refugees, the true cost of fast fashion, and international NGO cooperation.
    • 高階語法：混合條件句（Type 2 & 3）、高級假設語氣（wish / as if）、條件句省略與高級倒裝句型。Grammar Mastery: Mixed conditionals, hypothetical pasts, omitted "if" structures, and advanced emphasis inversions.
  • Phase 6：思辨發表與高中銜接複習 (Lessons 15-17 | Dec 21 - Jan 4)
    • 主題思辨：IBDP 寫作體裁（Paper 1）全真模擬、個人世界議題口說發表（IO Presentation）、高中知識論（TOK）哲學啟蒙。Global Themes: Paper 1 Text Handling, Individual Oral (IO) Simulation, and introduction to Theory of Knowledge (TOK).
    • 高階語法：會考高分盲點與陷阱題總複習、藍本 Azar 診斷測驗、高中語法斷層銜接。Grammar Mastery: Comprehensive review for CAP exam traps, Azar diagnostic testing, and high school grammar transition.

📊 彈性延伸單元 (Lessons 18-19 | 獨立引導與精修)：此兩堂課可視學生狀況，靈活調整為「全真會考英語文法題庫衝刺」或「個人英語思辨寫作作品集（Portfolio）一對一深度編修」。
Flexible Buffers: Tailored to student needs—can be utilized as intensive CAP mock exam drills or one-on-one portfolio feedback.
